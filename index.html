📖 Cornell Notes

Easy revision format for technical interview preparation

Q1 • CORNELL NOTES

What is a Laboratory?

🔑 Cues / Key Points

Samples • Testing • Diagnosis • Laboratory

📝 Detailed Notes

A laboratory is a facility where clinical samples such as blood, urine and other body fluids are examined using appropriate laboratory methods.

💬 Short Interview Answer

A laboratory is a facility where patient samples are tested to help in diagnosis and monitoring.
