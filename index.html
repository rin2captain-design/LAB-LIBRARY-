Q1. What is a Laboratory?

🔑 Cues / Key Points

Testing • Samples • Diagnosis • Laboratory

📝 Detailed Notes

A laboratory is a place where clinical tests and scientific examinations are performed on patient samples.

💬 Short Interview Answer

A laboratory is a facility where patient samples are tested to help in diagnosis and monitoring.
